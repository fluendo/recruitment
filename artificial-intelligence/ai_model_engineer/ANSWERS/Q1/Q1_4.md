# Q1.4 — Dataset not improving after adding bald people imagery

We added bald people images to the dataset, but accuracy did not improve. The possible causes and how to fix them:

1. **Insufficient quantity:** The number of added images may be too small compared to the rest of the dataset. A few dozen images will not move the needle when the original dataset has thousands. We need to verify that the representation is proportional.

2. **Labeling quality:** The new images may have inconsistent or incorrect bounding boxes. If the model learns from bad examples, it will not improve. We should review the annotations of the new data for consistency.

3. **Distribution mismatch:** The new images may come from a different context (resolution, angles, lighting, background) than the rest of the dataset. If they do not match real inference conditions, the model will not generalize from them.

4. **Improvement is masked:** As discussed in [Q1.3](Q1_3.md), if we only look at global accuracy, a small improvement on bald people may be diluted and not visible. We should check group-segmented metrics to confirm whether there is any actual improvement.

5. **Not a data problem, but a feature limitation:** Bald faces lack a key visual cue (hair) that the model may rely heavily on for face localization. If the model depends strongly on hair to detect faces, adding more data will not solve an architectural limitation. In that case, we need to revisit the model's approach — more examples alone will not fix it.
